A premier platform where industry leaders, experts, and stakeholders converge to explore, discuss, and shape the future of India's thriving hair economy.

About the Summit

The Indian Hair Economy Summit is more than just a conference; it's a movement aimed at recognizing and elevating the Indian hair industry's global standing.

Our goal is to foster collaboration, promote ethical practices, and drive innovation within the sector, ensuring sustainable growth for all stakeholders involved in this intricate ecosystem.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the IHESUMMIT? expand_more

The Indian Hair Economy Summit is a premier conference bringing together global leaders to discuss the growth, ethics, and innovation within the Indian hair industry.

Who can attend the summit? expand_more

The summit is open to salon professionals, manufacturers, exporters, investors, policy makers, and anyone with a professional interest in the hair economy.

Where is the venue for the summit? expand_more

The summit will be held at a premium convention center in New Delhi. Registered delegates will receive full venue details and travel assistance guides.

How can I sponsor the event? expand_more

Please fill out the 'Register Interest' form selecting 'Sponsorship' or contact our partnership team directly at support@ihesummit.com.

Are certificates provided for workshops? expand_more

Yes, all participants in the technical workshop tracks will receive official certification recognized by our partner industry bodies.
